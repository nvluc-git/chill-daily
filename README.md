# Chill Daily - Digital space helps with Work and Relaxation

Chill Daily is an application designed to create a calm, inspiring workspace that balances productivity with comfort — allowing you to stay focused while enjoying every moment.

Whether you're a freelancer, a student, or simply someone who wants to turn their workspace, study corner, or relaxation spot into a more pleasant and personalized environment, Chill Daily is built for you.

![Chill Daily](/public/imgs/intro.png)

## Features

💻 Focus Mode — Optimize your screen space for deep focus.

🖼️ Background Customization — Personalize your interface with images that match your mood.

📺 YouTube Video Integration — Watch educational or entertainment content directly in the app.

📝 Notes & Rich Text Editing — Quickly capture ideas with a customizable text editor.

✅ Task Management — Plan your day, track your progress, and accomplish your goals.

⏱️ Smart Timer — Boost productivity with Pomodoro sessions or countdown timers.

🎵 Ambient Music Player — Relax and concentrate with soothing playlists while you work.

🌦️ Weather Display — Stay updated on the current weather and forecasts to better plan your day.
